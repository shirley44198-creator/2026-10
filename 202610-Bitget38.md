<h2>2. What Is Bitget Copy Trading?</h2>

<p>The principle of copy trading: you select a trader, and the system automatically replicates their open, close and position adjustment actions proportionally.</p>

<p>Meaning for beginners:</p>
<ul>
<li>No need to watch the market 24 hours; the trader executes the strategy;</li>
<li>No need to judge entry/exit timing yourself;</li>
<li>You can observe multiple traders' public track records before deciding.</li>
</ul>

<p>But you must establish the right expectation first: Copy trading is not principal-protected, nor is it handing your money to someone else to keep. Positions remain in your account, and you bear the profit and loss from your own funds. A trader's historical performance does not represent future results.</p>

<h2>6. How to Set Copy Trading Risks?</h2>

<p>Before starting, confirm each of these:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Copy amount:</strong> Only use funds you can afford to lose; don't go all-in on a single trader;</li>
<li><strong>Stop-loss line:</strong> Set an account-level stop-loss that automatically stops when triggered;</li>
<li><strong>Leverage multiple:</strong> Don't blindly copy the trader's high-leverage setting;</li>
<li><strong>Diversification:</strong> Follow 2-3 traders with different styles simultaneously — safer than betting heavily on one;</li>
<li><strong>Regular review:</strong> Check performance weekly; stop-loss and exit if it deviates from expectations.</li>
</ul>

<p>⚠️ Not checking your account after copy trading is one of the most common causes of losses. When the market style switches, yesterday's champion trader may suffer consecutive drawdowns.</p>

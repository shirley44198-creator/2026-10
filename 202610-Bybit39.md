<h2>4. Bybit Futures Trading Introduction</h2>

<p>Before starting futures trading, understand these key concepts:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Leverage:</strong> the multiplier that scales trading size; Bybit supports up to 100x</li>
<li><strong>Long:</strong> you believe the price will rise, so you buy to open a position</li>
<li><strong>Short:</strong> you believe the price will fall, so you sell to open a position</li>
<li><strong>Stop loss:</strong> a preset price at which the position closes automatically to limit losses</li>
<li><strong>Funding rate:</strong> longs and shorts pay each other every 8 hours to balance the market</li>
<li><strong>Margin:</strong> your own capital; if losses reach a certain level, the position is forcibly liquidated</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Beginner tips:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Practice with 2-5x low leverage first</li>
<li>Set a stop loss every time you open a position</li>
<li>Don't go all-in; keep sufficient margin</li>
<li>Start with a demo account, then use real funds</li>
</ul>
